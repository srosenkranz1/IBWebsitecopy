What is IB?

The International Baccalaureate, in plain terms.

Sunny Hills has been an authorized IB World School since 1985.

The International Baccalaureate Diploma Programme is a two-year course of study, undertaken in the junior and senior years and recognized by universities worldwide. It is not an assortment of unrelated honors classes but a single, coherent program, designed to develop students who can conduct research, write with precision, and reason independently.

Students pursue six subjects drawn from across the major disciplines and unite them through three shared components: Theory of Knowledge, the Extended Essay, and Creativity, Activity, Service (CAS). The aim is not breadth of coverage for its own sake, but genuine depth, and the intellectual confidence to engage questions that admit no single, tidy answer.
