Results & University Recognition

The Sunny Hills Class of 2026 exam results, and how colleges and universities recognize the IB Diploma.

The 2026 May exam session

Thirty Sunny Hills students sat the Diploma Programme exams in the May 2026 session. Their results show a program that pairs rigor with support.

28 of 30candidates earned the IB Diploma
95.3%of exams passed (163 of 171)
23 of 30earned 30 points or more
31average total score (out of 45)
  • Strong exam results. The 95.3% pass rate is the best of the years we compared: 91.1% in 2020–21, 92.9% in 2024–25, and 95.3% in 2025–26.
  • High individual scores. The top total score was 38 points, and several students earned 36. A total of 30 or more is a key mark for sophomore standing at many universities.
  • Perfect pass rates. Nine of the 14 subjects tested had a 100% pass rate: English A: Literature HL, Mandarin B SL, Korean B HL, Korean B SL, Spanish B SL, Economics SL, History of the Americas HL, Physics SL, and Visual Arts HL.
  • Above the world average. Eight of the 14 subjects scored above the worldwide IB average: English A: Literature HL, Korean B SL, Spanish B SL, History of the Americas HL, Biology SL, Physics SL, Mathematics: Analysis and Approaches SL, and Visual Arts HL.
  • College outcomes. Graduates earned admission to several University of California campuses, and three students were accepted to Ivy League universities.

How universities recognize the IB

Most prominent universities in the United States have recognition policies for IB Diploma and Certificate candidates, and thousands of institutions worldwide recognize the IB. Recognition can include advanced placement, course credit, advanced standing, and special consideration at the time of admission. These policies exist largely because universities have had positive experiences with IB students and value the challenge the program offers.

Each university sets its own admissions criteria and its own terms for recognizing advanced programs, so families should check the policies of each college their student is considering. In general, grades of 5, 6, or 7 on Higher Level exams usually earn credit or advanced standing, while Standard Level exams generally do not.

Sending IB transcripts

Universities require an official IB transcript in addition to the high school transcript. With help from the IB Coordinator, students submit a transcript request to the IB to have their IB results forwarded to the university of their choice.
