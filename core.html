The Diploma Programme

The DP Core

Theory of Knowledge, the Extended Essay, and CAS — the three requirements that connect the six subjects and broaden the educational experience.

Theory of Knowledge (TOK)

Theory of Knowledge encourages each student to reflect on the nature of knowledge by critically examining what it means to know. Students explore core themes and different kinds of knowledge — scientific, artistic, mathematical, and historical — analyze concepts and arguments, and develop a defined basis for value judgments.

At Sunny Hills, TOK is integrated into the junior-year English HL course and the senior-year History of the Americas HL course. Together with the Extended Essay, TOK can add up to 3 points to a student’s Diploma total.

The Extended Essay (EE)

The Extended Essay is a substantial piece of independent research: an in-depth study of a question in one of the subjects the student is taking, written under the direct supervision of a qualified mentor from the Sunny Hills teaching staff. The IB maximum is 4,000 words, and the project typically takes about 40 hours spread across the two years.

Students choose a subject offered in the school’s curriculum, preferably one they are studying at Standard or Higher Level. Extended Essay support is built into the TOK class in the spring semester of junior year.

Creativity, Activity, Service (CAS)

CAS asks students to learn from the experience of doing real tasks beyond the classroom. Students may combine creativity, activity, and service in a single project or pursue each separately. Activities are approved under IB guidelines, with a balance across the three strands, and completion is judged by the quality of the activities and learning rather than the number of hours logged.

CAS is a counterbalance to the academic side of the Diploma: it invites students to share their energies and talents, build awareness and concern for others, and learn to work cooperatively — in the school, local, national, and international communities. Satisfactory CAS participation is required to earn the Diploma.
