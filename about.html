The Diploma Programme

About the IB Diploma Programme

A two-year, internationally recognized course of study that balances breadth and depth — and prepares students for university and life beyond.

What the Diploma Programme is

The IB Diploma Programme is an academically challenging and balanced program of education with final examinations, designed for students normally aged 16 to 19. It is normally taught over two years and has earned the recognition and respect of the world’s leading universities.

Since the late 1960s the program has balanced subject breadth and depth and has asked students to consider the nature of knowledge across disciplines through the unique Theory of Knowledge course.

  • International-mindedness, starting from a foundation in students’ own language and culture.
  • A positive attitude to learning that prepares students for university education.
  • Rigorous external assessment against published global standards, which makes the qualification welcome at universities worldwide.
  • The whole student — physical, intellectual, emotional, and ethical development.

How the two years are structured

Diploma students study six courses, choosing one subject from each of the six subject groups — languages, individuals and societies, the experimental sciences, mathematics, and the arts (or a second subject from another group). At least three and no more than four are taken at Higher Level (HL, recommended 240 teaching hours); the rest are taken at Standard Level (SL, 150 teaching hours).

At Sunny Hills, SL exams are taken in both the junior and senior years, while HL exams are taken in the senior year only. Most HL courses begin junior year and finish senior year. See the full course catalog.

Three requirements, known as the core, tie the six subjects together:

How the Diploma is scored

  • Each course is scored from 1 (lowest) to 7 (highest). Written exams are marked by external IB examiners; in-school assessments are marked by teachers and moderated externally, or sent directly to external examiners.
  • Up to 3 additional points are awarded for combined results in Theory of Knowledge and the Extended Essay, so the highest possible total is 45.
  • The Diploma is awarded to students who earn at least 24 points, meet minimum performance levels across the program, and satisfactorily complete CAS.
  • Assessment is criterion-based: students are measured against published criteria, not against other students taking the same exam.

Students who prefer not to pursue the full Diploma can take individual IB courses and earn subject certificates instead.

The IB learner profile

The ten qualities of the IB learner profile inspire the work of teachers, students, and schools. IB learners strive to be inquirers, thinkers, communicators, risk-takers, knowledgeable, principled, open-minded, caring, balanced, and reflective.

The path at Sunny Hills

Students begin in the Sunny Hills Honors/Pre-IB sequence in 9th and 10th grade, then enter the Diploma Programme in the junior year. Continuation is based on student performance and teacher recommendation each semester. In the spring of 10th grade, qualified students decide whether to pursue the Diploma and meet with the IB Coordinator and their counselor to plan their final two years. 8th grade Pre-IB/Honors admission · Frequently asked questions

Our community

Rigor with support means more than classwork. IB students at Sunny Hills build friendships through shared dinners, beach bonfires, and other gatherings that make the two years feel like a team effort.
